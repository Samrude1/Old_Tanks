# Tank Battle Cartoon - Project Information

## About This Project

This is a modern recreation of the classic **Scorched Earth** artillery game, reimagined with cartoon-style graphics, intelligent AI opponents, and enhanced gameplay mechanics.

## Original Scorched Earth (1991)

Scorched Earth is a shareware artillery video game released for MS-DOS in 1991, originally written by Wendell Hicken using Borland C++ and Turbo Assembler. Players control tanks to do turn-based battle in two-dimensional terrain, adjusting the angle and power of each tank turret before each shot. Scorched Earth added destructible terrain and a variety of extreme weapons to the artillery formula.

### Inspiration

The original was inspired by the ZX Spectrum game "Tanx" created by Paul Stanley and published in 1983 as a type-in in the book "Sixty Programs for the Sinclair ZX Spectrum".

### Classic Gameplay

Scorched Earth is one of many games in the genre of "turn-based artillery games". Such games are among the earliest computer games, with versions existing for mainframes with only teletype output. Scorched Earth, with a plethora of weapon types and power-ups, is considered the modern archetype of its format.

The game had a wide variety of customization options from gravity, wind, money, meteorite showers, and a similarly large pool of different payloads, allowing for a large amount of entirely different situations.

The AI players could display text messages before firing, such as "I shall smash your ugly tank!" and before dying, such as "Join the army, see the world they said".

### Classic Weapons

The weapons ranged from small missile rounds to MIRV warheads to high-yield nuclear weapons. All weapons could be upgraded with tracers which allowed the player to more accurately adjust the trajectory on their next turn. In addition to conventional warheads, there was also ordnance such as napalm, wildly bouncing bombs, and earth weapons - allowing the player to dump dirt on other tanks or to remove ground from beneath them.

## This Modern Recreation (2025)

### Key Differences

**Visual Style**
- Cartoon-style graphics with vibrant colors
- Smooth animations and particle effects
- Three themed environments (Normal, Desert, Winter)
- Unique visual design for each projectile type

**AI System**
- Intelligent bot with learning capabilities
- Four difficulty levels with distinct personalities
- Strategic weapon selection based on situation
- Obstacle detection and trajectory optimization

**Game Modes**
- Quick Play with customizable settings
- Campaign mode with progressive difficulty
- 6 challenging levels to master

**Weapons Arsenal**
- 8 unique weapon types with distinct mechanics
- Animated projectile visuals
- Rarity-based ammo system
- Manual cluster bomb splitting

**Technical Implementation**
- Modern ES6 JavaScript modules
- Modular architecture (13 separate files)
- Optimized for browser performance
- Delta-time physics for consistent gameplay

### Architecture Highlights

The game has been refactored from a single 1727-line file into a clean modular structure:

- **Physics Engine** - Realistic ballistics with gravity and wind
- **AI Bot** - Learning system with iterative shot correction
- **Particle Systems** - Explosions, debris, smoke, and effects
- **Terrain Generation** - Procedural landscapes with destruction
- **Rendering Pipeline** - Optimized canvas-based graphics

### Development Timeline

- Requires modern browser with ES6 module support
- Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (latest versions)

- 60 FPS target on modern hardware
