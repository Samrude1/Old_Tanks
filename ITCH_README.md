# Tank Battle Cartoon! 🎮

Turn-based artillery combat with smart AI opponents and cartoon-style visuals. Inspired by the classic **Scorched Earth**.

## Features

- **4 AI difficulty levels** - From Rookie to Elite
- **Campaign Mode** - 6 progressive levels
- **8 unique weapons** - Cluster bombs, napalm, MIRVs, and more
- **3 themes** - Normal, Desert, Winter
- **Dynamic physics** - Wind and gravity affect every shot

## Controls

- **A/D** - Aim turret
- **W** - Hold to charge, release to fire
- **S** - Cycle weapons
- **R** - Restart

## How to Play

Aim your turret, adjust for wind, and destroy the enemy tank before they destroy you. The AI learns from each shot, so adapt your strategy!
