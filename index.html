⚔️ Battle Controls

🟢 Player 1 (Green)
🎯 A/D - Aim turret
🚀 W - Charge & Fire
🔄 S - Switch weapon

🎯 ←/→ - Aim turret
🚀 SPACE - Charge & Fire
🔄 TAB - Switch weapon
