Scorched Earth - Two Player Battle

Controls

Player 1 (Green)
A/D - Aim turret
W - Charge & Fire
S - Switch weapon
Player 2 (Red)
←/→ - Aim turret
SPACE - Charge & Fire
TAB - Switch weapon

Weapons: Regular • Cluster • Bouncing • Heavy
Cluster Bomb: Press fire button again mid-flight to split!
R - Restart Game
